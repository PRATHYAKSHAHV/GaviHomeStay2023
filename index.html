About Us

Welcome to GaVi Homestay

Nestled near the serene Thunga River in Sringeri, Chikkamagaluru, GaVi Homestay offers a warm blend of comfort and nature. Our stay is designed for families, pilgrims, weekend travelers, and guests who want simple hospitality in a calm setting.

Enjoy comfortable rooms, home-cooked food, scenic surroundings, and easy access to Sringeri Sharada Peetham, riverside viewpoints, and local nature experiences.
